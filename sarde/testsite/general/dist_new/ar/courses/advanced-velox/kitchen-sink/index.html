    Footnotes

    Velox uses a radix tree1 for routing, which provides $O(k)$ lookup time where $k$ is the length of the key2.

    1. Also known as a Patricia trie or compact prefix tree. ↩︎

    2. In practice, the key length is bounded by the maximum URL path length. ↩︎
