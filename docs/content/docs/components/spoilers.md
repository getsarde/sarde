Spoilers hide inline text until the reader hovers or clicks on it. The text appears blurred until revealed.

## Basic Usage

Wrap text with double pipes `||` to blur it until hovered or clicked:

```md
The answer is ||42||.
```

Renders as: The answer is ||42||.

## Example

```md
The killer was ||the butler||. The treasure is hidden at ||coordinates 42.3, -71.1||.
```

The killer was ||the butler||. The treasure is hidden at ||coordinates 42.3, -71.1||.

Inline spoilers work for quiz answers, plot details, or anything the reader may prefer to reveal at their own pace.
